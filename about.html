Our story

A small family business that takes pride in every job

Five Star Home Improvement is a family-run business based in Dublin, built on many years' experience in home improvement, gardens and property maintenance.

From a quick repair to a full garden makeover, a new roof or a freshly tiled floor, we treat your home as if it were our own. You deal with us directly from the first call to the final tidy-up.

Because we cover the garden, the outside of the house and the inside, you don't need to find a different tradesperson for every job. One call to us gets it sorted.
